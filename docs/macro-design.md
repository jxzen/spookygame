## The experience the game is for (draft, inference)

The player should feel like **a lone, capable agent far from home in a world that has gone wrong**:
- **Epic** in the moments that open and close a mission (director: "epic like a james bond intro").
- **Dread** between them: the supernatural is real, and the player is in places they do not belong (Alan Wake, for horror).
- **Wonder** at the other worlds: painted, strange and beautiful even when dangerous (Spirited Away, for art).
- **Homesick resolve** across the whole arc: every jump between worlds is a step towards getting home.

The tension to resolve in design: epic spy swagger and slow horror dread pull in opposite directions. The pacing
reference picks (below) decide how the game alternates between them.

## Premise (director's, restated)

- Decades after a nuclear catastrophe, the world is in economic and political turmoil, and supernatural beings walk in it.
- The player is an American spy stranded abroad, trying to get back home.
- The player can teleport between dimensions or worlds. This is the hero power. Its rules are undecided.

## Core loop

Undecided by rule: mechanics come after the feeling and the reference picks. Open questions the picks will answer:
- Is the dimension jump the main verb (used constantly, like a traversal tool) or a rare, costly act (used at set pieces)?
- Is spycraft stealth, social infiltration, gadgets, or combat?
- Is the horror avoid-and-hide, or fight-with-light (the Alan Wake model, recall)?

## Update after batch 1 (2026-11-12, director's words verbatim)

> **t1** kbm support or controller.
> **t2** Focus on only making the first mission fit into a 5-10 minute gameplay chunk
> **t3** make it happen more rarely, like a dimension shift in inception.
> **t4** spy action with horror interludes
> **t5** yes i meant neuromancer, and blake crouch's novel.
> **t6** unreal engine is the end goal but build it for prototytping for now. the theme is director's cut, so make it extra epic

What changes:
- **Scope** is one mission of 5–10 minutes. With that length, the first five minutes and pacing cover most of the game.
- **Tone** resolves to spy action as the base, with horror as interludes. The epic peaks are the title sequence and the dimension shift.
- **The dimension shift is rare**: one or two set-piece moments in the mission, not a traversal verb.
- **Jam theme "director's cut"**: make it extra epic.

Picked readings: title sequence = Skyfall main titles; environment = Spirited Away; music = Paul's Dream (Dune).
