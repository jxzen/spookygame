# Hints: Ghostwire: Tokyo main theme

## Mechanism rules we take
1. **The Japanese pluck is a thread, not a guest.** The high plucked layer runs through quiet and loud sections alike. Our koto appears in every state (the density varies), so it reads as part of the score rather than a sound effect on top.
2. **Traditional scale plus modern bed, one tonic.** Yanagi mixes traditional scales with synths and noise (fetched). The mix holds together because the E bass never moves. Our koto's miyako-bushi scale on D is a subset of the strings' D Phrygian, so the two cannot clash.
3. **Stepped build in three tiers.** Plucks alone, then low sustain, then low block plus pulse. These map to our intensity thresholds (koto/pad, then bass and spiccato, then braam and pulse).
4. **Leave it unresolved when it matters.** It ends away from E. Our hide and castle states avoid the full Eb-to-Dm cadence; the explore and escape states have it.
5. **Watch the master.** The reference hits +0.4 dBTP. We hold true peak at or below -1 dBTP with a limiter and headroom.

## What not to copy
- No melody, no gagaku wind timbres imitated note for note, no nursery-song quotes. Never ship the audio.
