# Hints: Null Moon

## Mechanism rules we take
1. **A half step above the tonic is the dread note.** The bass leaning between C and Db makes a stable key feel watched. In D that note is Eb. It becomes our threat colour (the Eb/D chord) and our cadence (Eb resolving to Dm).
2. **Low end and pulse arrive and leave as one block.** At 8 s and 124 s the bass and beat enter and exit together, on a structural boundary. In our score the low pulse layer switches on bar lines, never by fading in mid-bar.
3. **One layer never stops.** The keys band runs for all 170 s. For us that is the D pedal and drone, present in every state.
4. **A pleasant surface over an uneasy bed.** The melody sounds pleasing while the bass does the harm. So the koto leitmotif stays lyrical and the strings' Eb/D carries the menace.
5. **About 74 BPM is a usable horror walking tempo**, close to our 72 BPM explore state.

## What not to copy
- No bell melody, no keys patch imitation, no drum pattern. Never ship the audio.
