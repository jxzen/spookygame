# Hints: Promise (Reprise)

## Mechanism rules we take
1. **One tonic in every window.** G is the top pitch class in all twelve 8-second windows. Our mission keeps D as the strongest pitch in every state, so the instruments always agree on where home is.
2. **One borrowed bright chord does the emotional work.** The Dorian major IV (i VI IV VI on Hooktheory) lifts a minor key without leaving it. We use one brightening of the mode, kept for the escape state.
3. **Two dynamic levels, not a ramp.** Thin intro, full body, thin outro (-20 / -13.5 / -20 S-LUFS). Our states change density in steps on bar lines rather than with continuous swells.
4. **The bed is an arpeggio that outlines the chord.** The pluck plays the harmony rather than decorating over it. That is our koto rule: it plays chord tones.

## What not to copy
- No melody, arpeggio pattern, chord-for-chord progression or tempo pairing from "Promise". We use i-VI only as a general minor-key cliché, inside our own D Phrygian plan.
- Never ship the audio.
