# Hints: Giacchino, "Light The Fuse"

## Mechanism rules we take
1. **Start the engine alone, then pile on.** 13 s of ostinato with an empty top before the orchestra. Our spy cue enters at intensity 0.45 (bass, kit, muted koto) and rises to 1.0 over 30 s: snare and brass from 0.5, the leitmotif from 0.6 (about 8 s in), octave doubling and braam from 0.8.
2. **Drop the top, keep the engine.** The 68-75 s break removes only the high band. In our cue the brass stabs rest in bars 2 and 8 of each 8-bar cycle so the lead's long notes breathe while the ostinato keeps going.
3. **Lean on a half step before landing.** D-Eb over the opening before G arrives. We use D-Eb in the bass (the Eb/D bar), which is also the horror score's dread colour: the spy cue grows out of the horror material.
4. **One pulse, two speeds.** 110 and 165 BPM coexist. Our score already does this with 72 (explore) and 144 (hunt, escape, spy).

## What not to copy
- The Schifrin theme, the fuse sound, the choir writing, any brass line. Never ship the audio.
