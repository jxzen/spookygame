# Hints (one mechanism per line)

- The end of every channel the player should take is lit; the channel itself is darker.
- A line (rail, pipe, cable) runs along the floor, wall or ceiling to the goal.
- One lamp over the door or object to use.
- Fences and closed shutters close the sides; one gap stays open.
- No marker on the HUD; the world carries the direction.

## What not to copy
- The Combine look, Xen growth, zombies and headcrabs, the teal grade, VR hands.
