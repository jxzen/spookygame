# Hints (one mechanism per line)

- A single saturated light in the direction of the goal pulls by contrast, even for a lost player (for us: red, the only colour).
- Darkness is a gate: an unlit opening says "not this way"; an adjacent lit place says "this way".
- Players drift to the safest-looking, best-lit place; put the way on there, or run a line from there to the way on.
- Long objects (rails, cars, cables) point the direction of travel; the goal sits framed at their end.
- Repeat one motif (a cable, a wire) along the whole route so it reads as "the way".
- Haze or light around the critical object, not everywhere.
- Turn heads with a sharp sound from the direction to look; a low rumble is too subtle.
- Show a landmark alone, framed, before the area that uses it.

## What not to copy
- Combine power shields, antlions, the Vault, headcrabs, resin, the gravity gloves.
- Teal/brown grade; holographic UI; voice lines that name what to do.
