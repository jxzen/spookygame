# Forbidden Siren: hints (one rule per line)

- Static on screen and in the ear means the hunter is close. [fetched 17:50] (This also answers the probe 03 note "use static more when the monster is close".)
- Let the player glimpse the hunter's own view (its light, its search) so they learn its routine without seeing its face. [fetched 18:10-18:30]
- A Japanese village at night reads through crooked lanes, stone walls, sheds and low fences, with sightlines under about 20 m. [fetched 10:10-12:10; widths inference]
- State the stage goal as a place at the edge of the map (the road out). [fetched 7:30]
- Lore objects are local and physical: a newsletter, a carved tablet, an idol by a well. [fetched 13:10-14:10, 24:00]
- Blood on a white wall is the trace, red on white. [fetched 19:10]

## What not to copy
- Trial-and-error stealth with instant failure; reviews call it tedious. [search only: GameSpot review]
- The multi-character timeline (Link Navigator); our scope is one character, one mission.
- Escort AI (Hisako); costly to build.
- A full sightjack mode as a separate verb; borrow the effect (a brief static view through its eyes) as a scripted moment, not a system.
