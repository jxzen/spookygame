# Opening horror levels: shared patterns (LEVEL-01, 2026-11-12)

Written after comparing the six studies in this folder: silent-hill-2-remake-opening, amnesia-tdd-opening, re7-guest-house, alien-isolation-first-encounters, pt-hallway, forbidden-siren-opening.
Labels: [fetched] = seen in our own footage or a fetched article; [search only]; [recall]; [inference]. A pattern is listed only if at least two fetched studies support it.

Format per entry: name, problem, rule, reason (evidence), good example, bad example.

## L1. The ladder of showing
- Problem: a monster shown early stops being scary ("this currently feels like slenderman", director, probe 03).
- Rule: climb one rung at a time, with at least about 45 s between rungs in a 5-10 min mission: words → sound → trace → far glimpse → shadow → part of the body → full body (once, in the last fifth).
- Reason: Amnesia goes words (p1 0:30) → note → flesh growth → voices → hide rule → hunt (p4 19:30) [fetched]. SH2R: 6 min of empty fog → far glimpse (18:00) → blood trail → contact (22:30) [fetched]. Alien: corpse → "I hear something" → slime → tail → gone (55:00-55:07) [fetched]. P.T.: one face at 75% of the run [fetched].
- Good: Alien's first reveal: 7 s, slime and a tail, never the whole body.
- Bad: probe 03: the stalker is fully visible from intel 1.

## L2. The first glimpse is far and walking away
- Problem: a monster facing the player turns every sighting into a chase.
- Rule: the first sighting is at the edge of visibility (about 30-35 m in fog), lit, crossing or walking away, and it does not notice the player.
- Reason: SH2R 18:00 [fetched]; Siren 36:00 (pale figure in a far doorway) [fetched].
- Good: SH2R's figure in the fog, followed by its blood trail.
- Bad: a monster that turns and charges on first sight.

## L3. Teach the hiding rule before the hunt
- Problem: a first hunt that the player does not understand feels unfair.
- Rule: state the rule in-fiction (a note, a radio voice, a tip) at least 60 s before the first hunt, then let the first hunt be survivable by following it.
- Reason: Amnesia's tip "stay out of sight and hide in the darkness... turn off your lantern" (p3 2:00) comes long before "find a place to hide" (p4 19:30) [fetched]. Alien's registration-point tip "they will warn you if enemies are nearby" (p1 14:00) [fetched].
- Good: Amnesia's two-stage tip.
- Bad: probe 03 never says what to do; the only answer is the focus-stun.

## L4. The first search misses
- Problem: if the first search finds the player, they learn hiding does not work.
- Rule: the first search of the mission always passes close (under 2 m) and leaves. Later searches can find the player, and then the player gets an exit.
- Reason: Grip at GDC Europe 2011, as reported by GameSpot: enemies come close to the hiding place and then move away [search only]. RE7's Mia tape: Marguerite's voice passes and leaves (43:15-49:15) [fetched]. Alien: designers mark spots the alien never checks [fetched article].
- Good: RE7's Mia tape.
- Bad: probe 03's "if it is blocking you it always gets you".

## L5. Escape needs geometry, not luck
- Problem: a monster that is faster than the player in a dead-end street cannot be escaped.
- Rule: every chase space is a loop (a block, or a building with two doors) of 50-80 m, the monster is slower than the player's run, and line-of-sight breaks (corners, doors, shutters) let the player get away. The monster never teleports during play.
- Reason: Alien: "two teleports, both for cutscenes" in the whole campaign [fetched article]; RE7 crawlspaces and corridors link rooms [fetched]; SH2R and Siren grids and lanes [fetched]. Monster speed relative to player run is [recall] for RE7 and Alien.
- Good: Siren's crooked village lanes with sheds.
- Bad: probe 03: one 5 m wide street with two dead-end spurs and a stalker that blinks forward 4-7 m.

## L6. The monster has a pressure valve
- Problem: a monster that never leaves exhausts the player and stalls progress.
- Rule: track menace (near plus in sight). When it peaks, the monster leaves for a set time by a route the player can hear (a roof, a drain), then comes back.
- Reason: Alien's director and menace gauge, backstage vents [fetched article]; Amnesia's long quiet after the first hunt (p5) [fetched].
- Good: Alien going into the vents.
- Bad: a monster that patrols forever.

## L7. Static and sound are the radar
- Problem: the player needs to know where the monster is without seeing it.
- Rule: one device turns proximity into static (sound and screen grain). It rises as the monster nears, and the monster's footsteps or voice carry its position.
- Reason: Siren full-screen static before sightjack (17:50) [fetched]; SH2R radio found at 25:00 [fetched frame; behaviour recall]; RE7 Marguerite's voice [fetched]; Alien motion tracker (p3 6:20) [fetched]. The director also asked for this (probe 03: "use static more when the monster is close").
- Good: Siren's static, then the hunter's own view.
- Bad: a silent monster (probe 03's stalker is shown, not heard).

## L8. The threat changes the space before it enters it
- Problem: a monster that only chases leaves the level unchanged (house rule H8).
- Rule: lights die, doors stand open, stains and traces appear where it has been: aftermath first, monster later.
- Reason: Amnesia flesh growth (p1 14:00) [fetched]; RE7 rotten kitchen (4:00) [fetched]; SH2R blood trail (18:20-20:50) [fetched]; P.T. decay per loop [fetched].
- Good: SH2R: the trail is also the critical path.
- Bad: traces with no direction.

## L9. A goal you can name, a way out you can see
- Problem: "feels like working towards something or you are actively trying to escape" (director).
- Rule: state the exit as a place early (a road, a station) and let the player see it before they can reach it. Each note moves the player one step closer to it.
- Reason: RE7 phone call: "a way out through the main hall" (32:15) [fetched]; Siren "Reach road to Karuwari" (7:30) [fetched]; Alien "Escape with Axel" (p1 54:20) [fetched].
- Good: Siren's objective card.
- Bad: probe 03: "INTEL 0/3" with no place to go until the gate appears.

## L10. Shops and rooms are side pockets off a spine
- Problem: a corridor level is boring; an open level gets lost.
- Rule: one readable spine (a street, a corridor) with enterable side rooms 5-10 m deep, each with a reward (note, item) and ideally a second door. Lore lives on walls and in objects inside them.
- Reason: SH2R's streets and the bar with "There was a HOLE here" (28:50) [fetched]; Siren's sheds and houses [fetched]; RE7's rooms off corridors [fetched]; P.T.'s one L-corridor with a bathroom pocket [fetched].
- Good: SH2R's bar interior.
- Bad: probe 03's shuttered fronts that cannot be entered.

## L11. Change a space the player already knows
- Problem: new spaces are expensive and do not exploit memory.
- Rule: send the player back through a learned space and change it (light colour, length, repetition). This is the cheapest and strongest form of the world shift.
- Reason: P.T. loops and the red light (9:05) [fetched]; Amnesia's white-outs over known rooms [fetched].
- Good: P.T. corridor turning red.
- Bad: probe 03's town morph that "appears for only a second and is confusing".

## What none of them do in the first 10 minutes
- Show the full monster within the first 15% of the run [fetched across all six].
- Let a monster blink or teleport during play [fetched: Alien article; others by observation].
