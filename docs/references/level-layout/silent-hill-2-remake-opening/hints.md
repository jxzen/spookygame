# Silent Hill 2 remake: hints (one rule per line)

- The first sight of the monster is far away, in fog, and walking away from the player. [fetched 18:00]
- A trace (blood, a stain, a drag mark) points where the monster went, and that trace is the critical path. [fetched 18:20-20:50]
- Roadblocks are dressed as fiction (barriers, "do not enter" signs) and turn a grid town into a guided route. [fetched 19:20, map 28:30]
- The first contact happens indoors, in the dark, after the player chooses to cross a threshold. [fetched 21:30-22:30]
- A sound device (the radio) warns of nearby monsters through static: sound before sight. [fetched 25:00 frame; behaviour recall]
- Lore sits in shop and bar interiors the player walks into, written on walls as well as on paper. [fetched 28:50-29:30]
- Long empty stretches build dread, but only when the fog hides the end of every street. [fetched 12:00-17:50]

## What not to copy
- The 6-minute empty walk: our whole mission is 5-10 minutes. Keep it to 60-75 s.
- Melee combat as the answer to the monster: our director wants hiding and escape, and a monster that appears late.
- The third-person camera.
- Creatures fought at mid range in the open at 27:00 and 30:00: frequent, cheap, fully visible monsters. Our monster is seen fully only once, late.
