# Resident Evil 7: hints (one rule per line)

- Open on aftermath (rotten food, a hand) before anyone hostile appears. [fetched 4:00]
- Show another victim's fate on found footage before the player meets the threat. [fetched 6:00-10:00]
- A searcher who talks out loud lets the player hide by ear alone: the voice is the map. [fetched 43:15-49:15]
- A stated goal ("a way out through the main hall") turns wandering into escaping. [fetched 32:15]
- Crawlspaces and hatches are thresholds that also break line of sight. [fetched 30:30-31:30]
- The red light (garage, 35:00) marks the violent beat; the rest of the palette is brown-grey. [fetched]
- Newspaper clippings carry world lore in one or two lines ("3 missing", "Over 20 missing in 3 years"). [fetched 28:15, 41:30]

## What not to copy
- The many full-body reveals (Mia, the family, Jack, a molded creature) in the first hour; our monster is shown once, late.
- Combat (gun, chainsaw boss); our mission is hide and escape.
- The dinner cutscene as the reveal: we never take the camera away from the player.
- Key and coin puzzles; no time for them.
