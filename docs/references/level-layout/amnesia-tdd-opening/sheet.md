## How the monster is hinted
Text first (p1 0:30), then a note that names it (p1 11:00), then a changed space (p1 14:00), then voices (p3 1:40), then the hide rule (p3 2:00), then the first hunt (p4 19:30). Six hints before the hunt. [fetched]

## Escape and hiding
- Hide in darkness, lantern off, behind furniture or in closets [fetched tip text; closets are recall].
- Design talk: Grip at GDC Europe 2011, as reported by GameSpot: enemies "wouldn't aggressively seek out hiding players but deliberately move close to their hiding place and then move away". [search only: the GameSpot snippet in search results; the article was not fetched]
- Frictional: "let players scare themselves", and the imagination effect "breaks down very, very fast". [fetched: gamedeveloper.com "four ways to design for horror"]

## Map shape
- Hub rooms with side rooms and corridors; doors are physical (drag to open), so doors are both a barrier and a way to listen [fetched tutorial tips; layout recall].
- Darkness doubles as cover: every dark corner is a hiding spot [fetched tip].
