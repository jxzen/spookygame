# Amnesia: hints (one rule per line)

- Name the threat in words before any image: a line of text or a note. [fetched p1 0:30, p1 11:00]
- Teach the hiding rule before the monster ever appears, so the first hunt tests a rule the player already knows. [fetched p3 2:00]
- The monster's traces change the space (flesh growth on a doorway) long before the monster is seen. [fetched p1 14:00]
- Sound with no source (voices, scrapes) is a cheap, strong hint. [fetched p3 1:40]
- Darkness is cover: the player hides by turning their own light off. [fetched tip]
- Screen-space effects (white-out, blur) tell the player their state without a meter. [fetched p1 17:30, p3 1:35]
- On a hunt, the monster comes close to the hiding spot and then leaves; it does not dig the player out. [search only: GameSpot on Grip, GDC Europe 2011]
- The rest after the first hunt is long and quiet (notes, no threat). [fetched p5]

## What not to copy
- The 77-minute wait before the first hunt; we have 5-10 min.
- Sanity as a meter that punishes looking at the monster; it adds a system we do not have time to teach.
- Physics puzzles (push objects, crank valves) as filler.
- Notes as walls of text: Amnesia's notes run about 80-120 words. Ours stay at 2-3 lines each (probe 03's notes were praised for lore).
