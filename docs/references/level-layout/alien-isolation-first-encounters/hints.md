# Alien: Isolation: hints (one rule per line)

- The first reveal shows parts only (slime from above, a tail tip) and lasts about 7 seconds. [fetched p1 55:00-55:07]
- Make the threat come from above: a drip, a sound in the ceiling, a vent. [fetched p1 55:00]
- A second person who says "I hear something" makes the player listen. [fetched p1 54:54]
- The monster never teleports during play; its menace comes from routes the player can learn (vents, corridors). [fetched: The Perfect Organism, "two teleports, both for cutscenes"]
- Run a menace gauge: when pressure has peaked, send the monster away so the player can act. [fetched: The Perfect Organism]
- The monster hears footsteps; running is loud, walking is quiet. [fetched: The Perfect Organism]
- Search hiding places in a believable, imperfect order; mark some spots it never checks. [fetched: The Perfect Organism]
- Unlock new monster behaviours as the mission progresses, never because the player died. [fetched: The Perfect Organism]
- After the reveal, give a long stretch with no monster on screen; the dread carries it. [fetched p2-p4]

## What not to copy
- A full AI with 100+ behaviour-tree nodes: we need about 5 states (wander, investigate, search, chase, leave).
- Instant death on contact: our probe 03 feedback says "if it is blocking you it always gets you". Contact must be escapable at least once (see the plan).
- The 41-minute wait before the glimpse.
- Hacking and crafting minigames.
