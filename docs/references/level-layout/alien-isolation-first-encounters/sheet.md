## How the monster is hinted
UI rule (14:00) → corpse (34:00) → vents taught (43:00) → "I hear something" → slime from above → tail, then pulled up out of view (55:00-55:07). Even the reveal shows parts only. [fetched]

## Escape and hiding (design write-up)
- Director AI with a "menace gauge": pressure rises when the alien is near or in sight; at the peak the director sends it away ("backstage", into vents) so the player can recover. [fetched: gamedeveloper.com "The Perfect Organism"]
- Senses: footsteps (walking vs running), noise; it searches areas of interest in a deliberately suboptimal order with backtracking; designers can mark places it never looks. [fetched, same article]
- Behaviours unlock over time; never because the player died. [fetched]
- Only two teleports in the whole campaign, both for cutscenes. [fetched] This is the opposite of probe 03's blinking stalker.

## Map shape
- Hub rooms joined by corridors and a parallel vent network: two layers, so the player always has a second route. [fetched vent crawl 43:00; network is recall]
- Lockers and desks as hiding spots. [recall]
