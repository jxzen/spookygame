# P.T.: hints (one rule per line)

- A small space the player learns, then sees changed, is scarier than a big new space. [fetched loops 1:20-2:45]
- Change one thing per loop (a door ajar, a light off, writing that grows). [fetched]
- Switch the light colour to red to mark that the rules have changed. [fetched 9:05]
- Lore written on walls grows as the player returns. [fetched 8:30, 10:00-10:35]
- One full reveal, late (about 75% of the way through). [fetched 16:30]
- A domestic object (phone, photos, a lamp) is the anchor the player watches for change. [fetched 0:55]

## What not to copy
- No escape verb: the director wants hiding and escaping.
- Opaque puzzles (P.T.'s final puzzle is notorious; recall).
- 23 minutes of loops; we use one return pass, not thirty.
- The face filling the screen as a jump scare is fine once; not more.
