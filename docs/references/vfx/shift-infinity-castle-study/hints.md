# Hints for our shift (from the Infinity Castle strips)

1. **The grid is the structure.** Even at full speed (iguro 0:01.3-1.8) the lanterns read as a regular lattice. In our lab the rooms sit on a 6 m grid and slide along visible black rails with red clamps, so the movement looks controlled, not random (the director: "the floating castle movements feel random").
2. **A musician moves the rooms** (recall). We map each koto string to a row: pluck string n and row n moves one cell. The note is the cause and the move is the effect, every time.
3. **Show one impossible thing for a long time** (trailer 0:26-0:46: a ceiling staircase held for about 20 s). Our gravity turn holds a table standing on the wall and a table on the ceiling; it does not stack ten tricks at once.
4. **Light streaks sell speed.** During the stretch our lanterns elongate with the corridor (the group is scaled), which echoes the lantern streaks.
5. **Roll is a punctuation, not a texture.** The roll in iguro 0:04-0:08 follows falling bodies. In first person we only turn gravity when the player walks into it.
