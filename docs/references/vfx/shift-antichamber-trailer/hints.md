# Hints (Antichamber)

1. **Turn around and it is different** (recall). This suits the loop: a change you only see when you look back. The lab does a version of this with the hall behind you, which stretches too, carrying the thing that follows you.
2. **Signs teach the rule.** A wall scroll or a red crease line can teach "this folds here" without a tutorial.
3. **Flat colour codes the rooms** (0:18-0:36, 0:52). Our palette is fixed (black, white, grey, red), so red is the only code: the exit, the creases, the strings.
4. Antichamber is calm and paced like a puzzle game. Borrow its spatial lies, not its pacing.
