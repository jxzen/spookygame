3. **Slow first, reveal once.** About 25 s of build, then one full overhead reveal (fetched). The director said probe 03's town morph "appears for only a second and is confusing". Give each effect a bar or more of build-up.
4. **Cut to a face = give the player a moment to look.** In first person that means a rest beat in which nothing moves, so the player can look up (inference).
