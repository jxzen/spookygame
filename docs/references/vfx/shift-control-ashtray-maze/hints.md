# Hints (Control)

1. **Music drives the architecture** (recall). This is the strongest precedent for our koto rule. Unlike Control, we make the beat-to-move mapping legible: one string moves one row by one cell.
2. **One material on every surface** (1:38-1:48) makes gravity ambiguous. We do the opposite on purpose: anything made of tatami is a floor, so the player can read where gravity will go.
3. **Pistons, not floating** (1:25-1:37). Slabs move on fixed axes and stop hard, so they read as machinery. Our platforms ease out over 0.4 s and stop exactly on the grid.
4. **The hatch of light** (1:46-1:53) is an exit in the floor. Our gravity turn ends at a red door in the ceiling you are walking on.
5. **Caution:** the Ashtray Maze is a power fantasy, with combat and a rock song. Ours is a horror escape, so we take the spatial reading and leave the triumph behind.
