# Hints (P.T.)

1. **The join must be invisible** (fetched 3:06 to 3:07: a door, then the same lit hallway, no fade). The lab renders three copies of the corridor (previous, current and next lap), placed by one rigid transform. The next lap is already visible through the end door before you step through it.
2. **One change per lap, and the player finds it.** Laps differ only in details: a clock hand, a tipped vase, an upside-down photo, a door left ajar with red light behind it. The player's eye does the work, and that is the psychological-thriller pressure.
3. **Escalate, then break the rule once.** Lap 3 adds a figure at the turn, lap 4 hangs the furniture on the ceiling, and lap 5 turns the door red and lets you out.
4. **Give the loop an exit the player can learn.** P.T.'s exit conditions were famously opaque (recall). Ours is audible: the koto adds one note per lap, and the phrase completes on lap 5. Walking backwards out of the start costs you a lap.
