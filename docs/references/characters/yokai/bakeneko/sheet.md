## Folklore
- A supernatural cat (kaibyo). The bakeneko has one tail; the nekomata has two (forked) and is more malicious. The Nabeshima bakeneko story of Saga is the most famous. [fetched: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakeneko; en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nekomata]
- Folk belief: a bakeneko licks lamp oil, which the *Wakan Sansai Zue* calls an omen of a coming strange event. Cats were seen as uncanny for their changing pupils, silent walk and nocturnal habits. [fetched: Wikipedia, Bakeneko]
- Mountain nekomata eat people (Fujiwara no Teika's *Meigetsuki*, 1233; Kenko's *Tsurezuregusa*, 1331). [fetched: Wikipedia, Nekomata]
- Buson (c.1754) and Sekien (1776) both draw cats **standing on their hind legs**, one with a towel on its head. [fetched: source/ images, viewed]
