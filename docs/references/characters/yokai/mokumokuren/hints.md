# Mokumokuren · build hints

- **The cheapest yokai to build:** a material on a shoji mesh. Each pane gets an eye decal with a lid blend shape, driven by a "gaze dot" (the angle between the camera's forward vector and the pane). Cost: one shader and one trigger.
- **Composition from Sekien:** the eyes fill the lattice grid evenly. The horror is the grid, not any single eye, so keep the pattern regular.
- **Light:** visible only in the falloff of the carried light, or when lit from behind (a light on the far side of the paper). Back-lit paper with eye silhouettes is a strong greyscale image.
- **Best use:** inside a hiding spot. The player is hidden from the monster, and something is watching them anyway.
