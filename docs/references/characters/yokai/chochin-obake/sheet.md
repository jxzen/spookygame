# Chochin-obake (提灯お化け, "lantern ghost"), Oiwa reading · sheet

## Folklore
- A yokai of the chochin (paper lantern): an old lantern splits to form a mouth with a long tongue, and grows one or two eyes. Pictured in kusazoshi prints, omocha-e and obake karuta from the Edo period on. Almost no oral legends exist, so the literature classes it as a yokai "that exists only in pictures". [fetched: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chōchin'obake]
- The serious line is **Oiwa**. Hokusai's *Oiwa-san* from his *Hyaku Monogatari* (One Hundred Ghost Stories) series was inspired by the kabuki play *Tokaido Yotsuya Kaidan* (1825), in which the murdered Oiwa's spirit appears out of a lantern. [fetched: same]
- In Hokusai's print the lantern's paper is the ghost's face: the lantern ribs run across a drooping eye, long hair hangs from it, and the paper is torn open below as a mouth. [fetched: source/hokusai-lantern-ghost-iwa.jpg, viewed]

## Glimpse spec
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Silhouette read target | **lantern with face** |
| Size | A real street or shop lantern, 40-60 cm tall, hung at head height or higher |
| Movement | None at first. Then a slow sway against the wind, and the torn "mouth" in the paper widens a little |
| Where in our level | A dead izakaya or noodle stall front; the lantern line along the shotengai (shopping arcade); a shrine approach |
| Trigger | The player's carried light flickers (lights should flicker, director, probe 03). The face shows only during the flicker frames |
| Duration | 2-4 flicker frames, about 0.3-0.6 s in total. When the light steadies it is a torn lantern again |
| Exit | When the light comes back, the face is gone. The lantern is still swinging though there is no wind |
| Trace left | The lantern swings for a few seconds, and the tear in its paper is now a shape |
| Sound cue | Paper creaking on its bamboo ribs. A breath with no source, timed with the flicker |

## Do not
- **Not the obake karuta version** (source/obake-karuta-1-08.jpg, kept as the bad example): a bouncing lantern with a cartoon eye and tongue is the comic reading.
- No tongue, no single cyclops eye, no hopping.
- Never use Oiwa's name, or a lantern with her name written on it (Hokusai's print carries a dedication). Take the image, not the character (see the roster's sensitivity notes).
- Red: an extinguished white lantern is our default. Lighting it red is the palette's "danger" signal. Decision for the director.
