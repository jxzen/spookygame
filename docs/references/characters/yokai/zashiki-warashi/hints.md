# Zashiki-warashi · build hints

- **source/ has no usable public-domain figure.** The Ugetsu Monogatari "spirit of money" print is only an archetype, and it shows an old-man figure (viewed). Design from the texts: a generic Edo-period child's kimono silhouette, short hair, bare feet. Keep it generic. A specific design needs a director pick.
- **Silhouette only:** black on grey, lit from behind by a window or the rain-light of the engawa. The face is never rendered.
- **The footprints are the payoff.** A decal trail in ash or soot, child-sized, ending at a wall. They work with the carried light: the prints show only when the beam sweeps the floor.
- **Narrative hook (inference):** if the town's zashiki-warashi left, the town fell. A child leaving through the town gate at the end of the mission would be a strong closing image. Decision for the director.
