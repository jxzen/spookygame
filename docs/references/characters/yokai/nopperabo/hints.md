# Noppera-bo · build hints

- **The light going out is the asset.** Hearn's story ends with "the light went out": this ties the yokai to the carried-light system. Script: reveal → force the light off for 1.5 s → light back, figure gone.
- **Spy-fiction disguise (inference):** the player expects a contact or informant at a stall (a spy beat). The noppera-bo sits in that contact's spot. The spy fiction sets up the scare. Decision for the director: is the real contact found later, or never?
- **Face material:** a blend from a neutral face to smooth skin over ~0.4 s as it turns. Keep subtle brow and cheekbone shapes under the skin, which is more disturbing than a flat egg.
- **Placement:** late in the mission (glimpse 5 or 6), after the player has learned that yokai vanish when looked at. This one does not vanish: it waits to be looked at. That breaks the grammar on purpose.
