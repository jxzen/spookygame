# Akaname · build hints

- **The pose is the asset.** Copy Sekien's composition literally. The figure is mostly hidden by architecture: only a hand gripping the corner post and a head at knee-to-waist height show. Model a full body anyway for the scuttle-out case, but author the glimpse as a corner-peek.
- **Light:** it sits just outside the cone of the carried light, and its wet skin catches one specular glint. In greyscale it reads as a pale shape low against a dark wall.
- **Animation:** two clips, `peek_idle` (the head tilts, 0.3 s) and `withdraw` (snaps back, 0.15 s). No walk cycle on screen.
- **Level:** put the sento alley early, as glimpse 1. It is ambiguous enough (a child? a dog?) to open the escalation.
- **Trace decal:** a clean streak through grime on a tile wall, at child height. Players who come back find it. This is the proof that they saw something.
