## Folklore
- A yokai that licks the scum and grime left in bathtubs and bathhouses. First named in Toriyama Sekien's *Gazu Hyakki Yagyo* (1776); an earlier form, *akaneburi*, appears in *Kokon Hyakumonogatari Hyoban* (1686). [fetched: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akaname]
- Edo depictions show a child-like figure with clawed feet and a cropped head, tongue out, at a bathing area. The *Nitto Honzo Zusan* calls it child-like with round eyes and a long tongue. The 1686 text says it is born from the negative energy (*inki*) of filth piled up in old bathhouses and derelict homes. [fetched: same]
- Sekien's plate shows it **half-hidden behind the corner of a bath hut**, with one clawed foot and one hand visible. [fetched: source/sekien-akaname-1776.jpg, viewed]
