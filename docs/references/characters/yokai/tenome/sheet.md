## Folklore
- From Sekien's *Gazu Hyakki Yagyo* (no text): a zato (a member of the guild of blind men) with eyes not on his face but on his palms. [fetched: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenome]
- *Shokoku Hyaku Monogatari* (1677): at the Shichijo-gawara graveyard in Kyoto, a man on a test of courage was chased by an old monster with an eye in each palm. He hid in a chest at a temple, there was a sound "like that of a dog sucking a bone", and the monk found him reduced to skin, with every bone gone. [fetched: same]
- Iwate tale: a blind figure on the plains at night, the eyes in its palms searching for something. [fetched: same]
- Sekien's figure stands in tall grass, face blank and lifted, hands raised with eyes in the palms. [fetched: source/, viewed]

## Do not
- **Do not make blindness the horror.** In the source the figure is a blind man; a game must not turn a disabled person into the monster. Drop the zato identity: a figure with no face detail and eyes in its palms.
- No close-up of palm eyes; they are two glints.
