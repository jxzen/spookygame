# Spy: build hints

## Rules
1. Red is only the tie and the hat band. No pocket square, no red lining, no red gloves.
2. The hat is the signature: keep a pinched crown and a front snap. It must survive a black-fill test at 64 px tall.
3. Coat stays open at the front below the belt so legs can stride through; keep it double-sided (single sheet + solidify).
4. Value stack: coat mid-grey, suit black, shirt white. Do not darken the coat to black; it then disappears in the fog world.
5. Faces: hollow, shaded sockets under the brim. Never show bright eyes; the brim does the noir work.
6. FP arms: right hand = flashlight (always), left hand free for interaction (reach). Coat sleeve, strap, white cuff, black glove, watch on the left wrist.
7. Under 15k tris (now ~4.6k + ~0.9k): spend the budget on cloth folds and the hat before anything else.

## What not to copy
- No actor likeness (Bogart or anyone): no face, hairline or build taken from a frame.
- No gun-barrel framing, no "Bond"/"007" names or logos; no cape or cowl shapes (a long coat flaring in wind must not read as a cape: keep it belted and sleeved).
- Nothing from Alan Wake 2 beyond general archetype (dark coat + flashlight): no FBI lettering, no Saga/Alan outfits, no trailer frames shipped.
- Trailer frames are studied, never shipped or traced.
