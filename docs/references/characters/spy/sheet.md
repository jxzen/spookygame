## Proportions (in heads; 1 head = 0.244 m, crown without hat = 1.83 m)
- Height 7.5 heads to the crown; hat adds ~0.3 head (top of crown ~1.94 m).
- Shoulder joints 1.52 heads wide (0.37 m); coat shoulders ~2.1 heads (0.50 m), slightly heroic, as in the 1940s padded trench.
- Waist (belt) at 4.5 heads down from the crown (z 1.09), cinched about 25% narrower than the shoulders.
- Crotch at 3.75 heads from the floor (half height). Knee 2.05 heads. Coat hem at 1.65 heads from the floor (mid-calf), like the Big Sleep trench.
- Fingertips reach mid-thigh (z ~0.70).
- Fedora brim is 2.3 head-widths across; it shades down to the cheekbones from a high key light.

## Silhouette notes
- Three shapes must read in black fill: the hat (brim wider than the shoulders' half-width, pinched crown), the shoulders (square, with epaulettes and a turned-up collar), and the coat flare (A-line from the belt, open at the front so the legs scissor in walk).
- Walk side silhouette is the strongest read (previews/spy_silhouette_walk_side.png): hat, stoop, flared skirt, stride.
- Front silhouette is still a column: arms hang close to the coat. Waist cinch + 13-15 degree arm splay opened a small gap (pass 3) but it is weak.
- Props break the silhouette asymmetrically: pistol low in the right hand, briefcase in the left.

## Value structure (black / white / grey / red; H5)
coat mid-grey (sRGB 0.34) > lapels/belt one step darker (0.26) > suit near-black (0.12) > shirt white (0.86) > tie red > hat near-black with red band > gloves and shoes black. Skin is desaturated grey (0.58), eye sockets 0.24.
Same stack as Big Sleep 00706: light trench, dark suit, white shirt V, dark tie (ours red, the only colour).

## Two-up notes (reference vs render), by pass
- Pass 1 (vs Big Sleep 01129, Maltese 01870): shoulders were a flat shelf with sleeve caps poking above; lapels were thin shards; the shirt V was one big white plate (the refs show a dark suit between coat and shirt); hem at the knee (refs: mid-calf); hat crown too low, like a pork-pie; face a white mask; coat over-lit and reading white.
- Pass 2: sloped shoulder rings + deltoid caps + epaulettes; wide notch lapels, double-breasted buttons; dark suit layer and narrow shirt V; hem to mid-calf; taller pinched crown, snap brim; lights dimmed. Title close-up showed the face still a mannequin and the tie knot floating off the shirt.
- Pass 3: sculpted head (brow ridge, sunken sockets coloured dark, cheekbones, square jaw, proper nose); tie re-seated on the shirt; waist cinched and arms splayed for negative space.
- Remaining differences: refs have cloth weight (folds, creases, the coat breaks over the belt); ours is clean low-poly. Refs' hats sit lower over the eyes; ours shows more face. Refs' faces are lit; ours relies on hollow dark sockets, which reads horror but less human.
