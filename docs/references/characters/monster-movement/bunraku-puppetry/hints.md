# Bunraku puppetry: hints

- Pose, hold, sharp accent, hold: the stillness stays alive because the head keeps micro-moving.
- Treat each limb as a separate operator: head, each arm and the legs are keyed on offset tracks so they never start or stop together.
- The accent is fast but eased in and out; it is a strike, not a pop to a new frame.
- Not to copy: costumes, the cherry-blossom set, the operators themselves, the stylised heroics of the play.
