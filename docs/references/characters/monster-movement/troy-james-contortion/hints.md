# Troy James contortion: hints

- Movement is continuous with real weight: the shoulder drops over each hand as it plants; nothing holds still on a stepped key.
- Hips are the highest point of the crawl; the head hangs below them.
- Wrongness comes from joint range: the spine rolls the torso about 180 degrees while hands and feet stay planted.
- Plants are quick (about 0.4-0.5 s per limb) in a four-beat walk; a gallop would read as an animal, this walk reads as a person.
- Planted hands and feet never slide (build implication: hand and foot IK locks).
- Not to copy: the stage, costume, the pauses played to the audience, the dance accents and the comedy.
