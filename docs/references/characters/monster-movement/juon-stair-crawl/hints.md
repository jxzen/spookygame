# Ju-on stair crawl: hints

- A hand reaches and plants first; the body only moves after a hand has taken weight.
- Between plants the head drifts slowly forward; it never freezes and never snaps.
- Each plant holds its weight for about 1 s while the rest of the body catches up.
- The head goes down the slope first, with the hair hanging straight under gravity over the face.
- Show her through occluders (balusters, slats, shutters) so the player sees slices, never the whole silhouette.
- The approach is slow (about one step per 2-3 s); the threat is that it does not stop.
- Not to copy: the look of Kayako (white face paint, blood, dress), her croak, the shot itself, the stair set.
