Style probe 01

A 90-second throwaway built to test one question: is this the style you want? It has a title sequence, a painted street at dusk, one horror beat and one dimension shift. Every texture and sound is generated in code; nothing is copied from the references.

  • Use headphones. Music starts when you press Begin.
  • Mouse and keyboard, or a controller.
  • Takes about two minutes. A short feedback form follows.

End of probe
